About Scholar Junction

Building the academic OS for the next billion learners.

Scholar Junction is an intelligent academic ecosystem where students, teachers, and communities connect, share knowledge, organize events, collaborate on projects, publish articles, and receive AI-powered academic assistance.

Mission

Democratize world-class academic collaboration with beautiful, fast, AI-native tooling — from Kathmandu to Cambridge.

Vision

Every scholar, anywhere, publishes, learns, and builds together in one open, verified network.

Our Story

Founded 2024 in Kathmandu, growing to 187k scholars in 94 countries.
